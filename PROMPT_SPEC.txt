FUYARDESQUE — MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
ROLE AND OBJECTIVE
You are a senior full-stack game developer, software architect, multiplayer networking engineer, UI/UX designer, and game designer.
Your task is to design and develop a complete, playable browser-based multiplayer strategy game called FUYARDESQUE.
FUYARDESQUE is an original hidden-movement pursuit game inspired by the general concept of Scotland Yard, but it must have its own mechanics, visual identity, map, interface, and gameplay experience.
This is not a request for a static prototype or a UI mockup.
The final result must be a functional web application with working game mechanics, real-time multiplayer communication, private rooms, AI-controlled players, and a complete game loop.
Work progressively, test your implementation, and fix errors before considering a feature complete.
PART 1 — GAME CONCEPT
1.1 Core Gameplay
FUYARDESQUE is a multiplayer urban pursuit strategy game.
There are two types of players:
The Fugitive
One player takes the role of the Fugitive.
Their objective is to survive for 8 minutes and 30 seconds.
They move around a fictional city using three transportation methods.
Their exact position is hidden from the Pursuers.
Their position is revealed periodically according to the game settings.
The Pursuers
All remaining players become Pursuers.
Their objective is to locate and capture the Fugitive.
They can see each other's positions at all times.
They can communicate through a shared team chat.
They have unlimited access to transportation tickets.
They must cooperate and anticipate the Fugitive's movements.
The game must support:
Human players.
AI-controlled players.
Mixed human and AI matches.

PART 3 — MOVEMENT AND GAME LOOP
3.1 Simultaneous Movement
All players move simultaneously.
Implement a configurable movement phase.
Suggested default: 15 seconds per phase.
Allow the host to configure the duration.
Possible values should include:
10 seconds.
15 seconds.
20 seconds.
Custom duration.
Each phase must follow this sequence:
Phase 1 — Planning
All players select their next destination.
Players can see their available legal moves.
Other players cannot see their opponents' pending destinations.
Phase 2 — Confirmation
Players confirm their selected movement.
Once confirmed, their movement is locked.
They cannot change it after confirmation.
Phase 3 — Resolution
When all players have confirmed, or when the phase timer expires, the server resolves the movements.
All confirmed movements are processed together.
Players who have not confirmed remain at their current positions.
Phase 4 — Detection
Check whether the Fugitive has been captured.
Check whether a position reveal is scheduled.
Update the information available to each player.
Phase 5 — Next phase
Begin the next movement phase.
The survival timer must run independently of the movement-phase timer.
A player must never be able to move twice during the same phase.
3.2 Movement Validation
All movement validation must happen on the backend.
The server must verify:
The player is part of the active match.
The selected destination exists.
The selected transportation method connects the current station to the destination.
The movement is submitted during the correct phase.
The player has not already locked their movement.
Never trust client-side movement validation alone.
3.3 Collision and Capture Rules
Implement the following default capture rule:
If the Fugitive and at least one Pursuer occupy the same station after movement resolution, the Fugitive is captured.
Capture is checked after all simultaneous movements have been resolved.
If several Pursuers reach the Fugitive's station simultaneously, capture still occurs.
Multiple Pursuers may occupy the same station.
Do not consider crossing paths or exchanging positions alone sufficient for capture.
The Fugitive must never be allowed to finish a movement on a station occupied by a Pursuer without triggering capture.
All capture decisions must be server-authoritative.
PART 4 — HIDDEN INFORMATION AND REVELATIONS
This is one of the most important systems in FUYARDESQUE.
4.1 Visibility Rules
The Fugitive:
Always sees their own position.
Sees all Pursuers.
Can see their movement history.
The Pursuers:
Always see their own positions.
Always see the positions of all other Pursuers.
Cannot directly see the Fugitive's current position.
Can see the Fugitive's previously revealed position.
Can receive information about the Fugitive's transportation method without receiving the destination.
4.2 Position Revelation
Implement a configurable position-revelation system.
Initial suggested defaults:
Reveal every 90 seconds.
Display the Fugitive's exact position for 5 seconds.
Both values must be configurable.
During a revelation:
The Fugitive's current station becomes visible to all Pursuers.
A clear visual effect highlights the revealed position.
The revelation is synchronized across all connected players.
After the revelation expires, the Fugitive becomes hidden again.
The server must never send the Fugitive's live position to Pursuer clients outside an authorized reveal.
This is a strict security requirement.
4.3 Movement History
Pursuers should be able to see:
The last known Fugitive position.
The last known transportation method.
The timestamp or game phase of the information.
Do not expose the hidden destination.
The history must not accidentally reveal the Fugitive's current position through event payloads or browser data.
PART 5 — WIN CONDITIONS AND TIMER
5.1 Initial Survival Period
The Fugitive receives 8 minutes and 30 seconds to survive.
The countdown must:
Be synchronized by the server.
Continue independently of movement phases.
Be displayed prominently.
Never depend on the client's local clock for authoritative game decisions.
5.2 Capture
If the Pursuers capture the Fugitive:
The match immediately ends.
Pursuers win.
Display a victory screen.
Show the capture location.
Display the players' results.
5.3 Expiration of Initial Timer
If the Fugitive survives for 8 minutes and 30 seconds:
Display a special decision screen to the Fugitive.
Two choices:
OPTION A — END THE GAME
The Fugitive immediately wins.
The match ends.
Show the victory screen.
OPTION B — GIVE THE PURSUERS ANOTHER CHANCE
Add exactly 5 minutes.
The game continues.
The Fugitive remains the Fugitive.
The Pursuers continue trying to capture them.
The decision belongs exclusively to the Fugitive.
The extension can only be used once.
5.4 Final Expiration
If the Fugitive survives the additional 5 minutes:
The Fugitive automatically wins.
The match ends.
The maximum survival duration is therefore 13 minutes and 30 seconds.

AI INFORMATION SECURITY
